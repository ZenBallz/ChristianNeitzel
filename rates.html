FAQ

Who owns the images

Under Austrian copyright law (UrhG), the photographer retains copyright in all images. What you purchase is a usage license.

Personal sessions include a license for unlimited personal use — your portfolio, social media and prints. Commercial use (advertising, campaigns, retail, print media) is licensed separately and depends on scope and duration.

Exclusive rights & buyouts

Exclusive usage is available and priced by scope, territory and duration. Time-limited exclusivity starts at 2× the production fee; full worldwide buyouts are quoted individually.

Note that under Austrian law copyright itself is not transferable — a buyout is an exclusive, unrestricted usage license.

Can I edit the photos myself

Please don't apply filters or re-edit delivered images — the edit is part of the work. Crops for platform formats are fine.

How and when do I get my images

You receive a private online gallery to make your selects within 5 days. Final retouched images are delivered within 10 days of your selection. Digitals are delivered within 48 hours.

Deposit, cancellation & rescheduling

A 25% deposit confirms your booking and is applied to the total. One free reschedule is possible if requested at least 48 hours before the shoot.

Cancellation more than 7 days before the shoot: deposit refunded minus costs already incurred. Within 7 days: 50% of the fee remains due. On the day, or no-show: the full fee remains due.

Revisions

One round of minor revisions per delivered image is included — a missed blemish, a crop adjustment. Stylistic re-grades or further rounds are billed hourly, agreed in advance.

Note that you book my editing aesthetic: all images are delivered in my signature style. Matching a different photographer's look is not part of standard editing.

What about raw files

Raw files are not delivered — the finished, edited image is the product.

Do you travel

Yes — available worldwide. Travel within Vienna is included in all rates. Beyond Vienna, travel time and costs are quoted per project.
